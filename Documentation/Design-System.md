# Steelbuild Brand Design System (SBDS)

Version: 1.0

Status:
Planning

Purpose:

To maintain a consistent, premium and scalable design language across the Steelbuild Infra Projects website.

# Chapter 1 – Brand Identity

## Brand Personality

Steelbuild Infra Projects is not just a steel construction company.

It represents engineering excellence, trust, innovation, precision, speed and long-term partnerships.

The website should communicate confidence, premium quality and international standards.

---

## Brand Keywords

- Premium
- Modern
- Industrial
- Engineering
- Trustworthy
- Reliable
- Innovative
- Strong
- Sustainable
- Scalable

---

## Design Goal

Every visitor should feel that Steelbuild is capable of executing large-scale industrial infrastructure projects.

The website should create trust within the first 5 seconds.

---

# Chapter 2 – Color System

## Primary Colors

Steelbuild Red: #C21119  
Used for buttons, highlights, CTAs, hover states and important actions.

Steelbuild Blue: #1B3F68  
Used for headings, navigation, footer, premium backgrounds and corporate identity.

Steel Gray: #8F8F8F  
Used for secondary text, borders, icons and supporting elements.

## Neutral Colors

White: #FFFFFF  
Used for clean backgrounds.

Light Gray: #F7F8FA  
Used for section backgrounds.

Dark Charcoal: #111827  
Used for main text.

## Color Usage Rule

Red should be used carefully and only for action or emphasis.

Blue should dominate the corporate identity.

White space should remain high to create a premium international feel.
