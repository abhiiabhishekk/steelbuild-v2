# Steelbuild V2 Design System

## 1. Brand Philosophy

Steelbuild V2 must communicate precision, scale and confidence.

The design should feel:
- Premium
- Industrial
- Modern
- Trustworthy
- Clean
- Engineering-focused

---

## 2. Colors

Primary Blue: `#1B3F68`  
Usage: headings, footer, dark sections, navigation, trust-building areas.

Primary Red: `#C21119`  
Usage: CTA buttons, highlights, hover states, badges.

Steel Gray: `#8F8F8F`  
Usage: secondary text, borders, icons.

White: `#FFFFFF`  
Usage: clean backgrounds.

Light Background: `#F8FAFC`  
Usage: alternate sections.

Dark Charcoal: `#111827`  
Usage: primary body text.

---

## 3. Typography

Heading Font: Manrope  
Body Font: Inter

H1: 72px  
H2: 56px  
H3: 36px  
Body: 18px  
Small: 14px  

Headings should be bold, confident and tightly spaced.

---

## 4. Layout Grid

Max Container Width: 1440px  
Desktop Grid: 12 columns  
Tablet Grid: 8 columns  
Mobile Grid: 4 columns  

---

## 5. Containers

Default container:
- max width: 1440px
- horizontal padding desktop: 32px
- horizontal padding mobile: 24px

---

## 6. Section Spacing

Desktop:
- Top padding: 160px
- Bottom padding: 160px

Tablet:
- Top padding: 120px
- Bottom padding: 120px

Mobile:
- Top padding: 80px
- Bottom padding: 80px

---

## 7. Border Radius

Cards: 32px  
Images: 32px  
Buttons: 16px  
Badges: 999px  

---

## 8. Shadows

Card Shadow:
Soft, premium and subtle.

Hover Shadow:
Stronger shadow with slight lift.

Avoid harsh shadows.

---

## 9. Buttons

Primary Button:
- Red background
- White text
- Bold font
- 16px radius
- Hover lift

Secondary Button:
- Border style
- Blue or white text depending on background
- Hover fill transition

---

## 10. Cards

Cards should use:
- 32px radius
- clean border
- soft shadow
- hover lift
- image zoom where applicable

---

## 11. Badges

Badges should be small, uppercase, highly spaced and used for categories, certifications and highlights.

---

## 12. Forms

Forms should feel clean and premium:
- Large input height
- Soft border
- Clear labels
- Strong submit button
- Mobile friendly

---

## 13. Icons

Icons should be minimal, line-based and consistent.

Avoid cartoon icons.

---

## 14. Animations

Motion should be smooth, subtle and purposeful.

Default duration: 0.6s  
Hover duration: 0.3s  
Easing: ease-out

---

## 15. Images

Use real Steelbuild images only.

No stock photos.

Hero images:
- 2560×1440
- WebP preferred
- Dark overlay required

Project images:
- 1600×1200
- WebP preferred

---

## 16. Responsive Breakpoints

Mobile first, but desktop experience should feel premium.

Desktop: 1280px+  
Laptop: 1024px–1279px  
Tablet: 768px–1023px  
Mobile: below 768px  

---

## 17. Accessibility

- Good color contrast
- Keyboard navigable buttons and links
- Proper alt text
- Semantic HTML
- Clear focus states

---

## 18. Motion Rules

Animation must never distract.

Animation should:
- Guide attention
- Improve storytelling
- Support hierarchy
- Feel premium
